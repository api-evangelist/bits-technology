# Bits
> Bits is a compliance infrastructure platform for regulated financial companies in Europe — covering KYC/KYB onboarding, AML, transaction monitoring, risk assessment, ongoing due diligence, and unified case management, with European data coverage. Available as a full stack or as a modular layer above existing providers.
Bits is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden. Customers are primarily payment institutions, neobanks, crypto/VASPs, and spend management companies across the Nordics, with expansion into DACH, UK and Benelux. Founded 2022 by alumni from Klarna, AWS, and Tink. Series A €12M (February 2026).
Two deployment models: full-stack for mid-market fintechs, and a modular orchestration layer (BYOV — Bring Your Own Vendors) for enterprises that want to keep existing data providers and add Bits as the workflow and intelligence layer above them.
## Platform
- [Homepage](https://www.bits.bi/): Overview of Bits — compliance infrastructure for regulated financial companies covering the full lifecycle from onboarding to ongoing monitoring.
- [KYC/KYB onboarding](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/onboarding): Business and individual onboarding with European registry coverage, UBO discovery, document verification, and eID.
- [AML case management](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/aml-case-management): AML screening, PEP/sanctions, adverse media, and unified case management with full audit trail.
- [Transaction Monitoring](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/transaction-monitoring): Real-time transaction monitoring for financial crime detection — fewer false positives, faster case handling.
- [Ongoing Due Diligence](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/ongoing-due-diligence): Automated daily monitoring across 1,300+ lists with configurable rules and event-driven review triggers.
- [Risk score](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/risk-score): Automated risk scoring and classification across customer relationships.
- [Credit score](https://www.bits.bi/use-cases/credit-score): Business credit scoring via Bits and Coface — 0–10 risk score, 5-year history, probability of default.
- [Bits AI](https://www.bits.bi/bits-ai): AI-powered compliance tools for automated decisions, investigations, and document analysis.
- [Integrations & data coverage](https://www.bits.bi/integrations): European registry data, eID providers, PEP/sanctions lists, and third-party compliance tools available within the Bits platform — covering multiple European markets.
- [Developers / API](https://www.bits.bi/developers): API documentation and integration reference for technical teams.
